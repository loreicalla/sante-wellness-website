1. Retail Bonus

HOW IT WORKS:
Buy qualifying SANTÉ products at the applicable member price, sell them at the applicable suggested retail price, and keep the qualifying retail profit.
💰 Sample Computation
Retail profit per box$15.20 USD
Boxes sold in one month50 boxes
$15.20 × 50 boxes =
$760.00 USD
Potential retail profit

Example: If you sell 50 boxes in one month, the sample retail profit is $760 USD. This could help contribute toward bills, groceries, savings, or other personal expenses.

Sample only: Actual earnings depend on actual sales, applicable pricing, discounts, expenses, and current SANTÉ program rules.

$500.00 USD

4. Leadership Bonus

HOW IT WORKS:
Develop leaders in your team. Once qualified, you may earn a percentage of their Infinity Bonus according to your rank and the applicable levels.
🏆 Sample Computation
Level 1: $2,000 × 20%$400
Level 2: $1,000 × 4%$40
Level 3: $800 × 4%$32
$400 + $40 + $32 =
$472.00 USD

Sample shown: This follows the example presented for a Platinum ED: Level 1 $2,000 × 20%, Level 2 $1,000 × 4%, and Level 3 $800 × 4%. Actual eligibility, percentages, levels, and payout are subject to the current compensation plan.
