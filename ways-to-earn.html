Bonus amounts, qualifications, eligibility, pricing and rewards depend on the applicable SANTÉ compensation plan, market and program rules. Contact Lore for guidance and the complete business overview.

1. Retail Bonus

Retail Bonus is connected to qualifying retail sales. The simple idea is to purchase qualifying products at the applicable member price, share or sell them to customers, and earn from qualifying sales based on the applicable program rules.

← Back to the 5 Ways to Earn

2. Fast Start Bonus

Fast Start Bonus is connected to qualifying activation packages purchased by Business Owners you personally sponsor. Qualification and eligibility are subject to the applicable program rules.

← Back to the 5 Ways to Earn

3. Infinity Bonus

Infinity Bonus is connected to building your Left and Right teams. When the required qualifications and matched business volume are met, this may create a bonus opportunity according to the compensation plan.

← Back to the 5 Ways to Earn

4. Leadership Bonus

Leadership Bonus focuses on developing leaders within your organization. As qualified leaders grow, you may become eligible to earn according to your rank and the applicable compensation plan.

← Back to the 5 Ways to Earn

5. Dream Quest

Dream Quest is a goal-based journey where you work toward milestones, level up, and unlock applicable rewards as you progress. Requirements and rewards depend on the current program rules.

← Back to the 5 Ways to Earn
