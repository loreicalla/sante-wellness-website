THE 5TH WAY TO EARN

Welcome to Dream Quest

Think of Dream Quest like a journey with levels. Complete the required goals, move up, and work toward the rewards connected to each quest.

1

Quest 1: Entry Level

Your starting point in the Dream Quest journey.

  • Who can qualify2-Star Business Owners and above; Director and up may earn multiple Quest bonuses.
  • Main goalsMaintain the required monthly activity and personally sponsor the required new 1-Star Business Owners.
Sample rewards: gadgets and luxury incentives.
2

Quest 2: Adventure Level

Reach bigger goals and work toward memorable travel experiences.

  • Who can qualifyQualified Executive Directors for the required period.
  • Main goalsMaintain the required monthly activity, personal volume, and sponsorship goals.
Sample rewards: regional, Asia, and Philippine travel incentives.
3

Quest 3: Milestone Level

Take your journey further and work toward a major lifestyle milestone.

  • Who can qualifyBusiness Owners who meet the required Gold Executive Director milestone and promo conditions.
  • Main goalsMaintain the required activity and reach the required personal volume.
Sample rewards: house or car incentives, based on qualification and available options.
4

Quest 4: Legacy Level

The highest Dream Quest level, designed for bigger milestones and global experiences.

  • Who can qualifyQualified Diamond Executive Directors who meet the required promo conditions.
  • Main goalsMaintain the required activity, volume, and sponsorship achievements.
Sample rewards: global travel slots, including options for a spouse at higher cycle qualification.
Important: Dream Quest mechanics, qualification requirements, rewards, points, and availability may change. Always refer to the latest official SANTÉ Dream Quest guidelines for the current details.
